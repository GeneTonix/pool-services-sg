FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you deploy a lifeguard? ⌄

We aim to respond to urgent requests as promptly as possible. Response time depends on availability and location. Contact us for same-day or next-day coordination.

What areas in Singapore do you cover? ⌄

We provide pool services across Singapore, including condominiums, private residences, commercial properties, and recreational facilities.

Do you offer one-time pool cleaning? ⌄

Yes. Our ad hoc pool maintenance includes one-time cleaning, weekly maintenance, emergency service, and vacation property maintenance.

Can you maintain our pool while we're on vacation? ⌄

Yes. Vacation property maintenance is one of our service options. We can schedule regular visits to keep your pool clean and safe while you're away.

What is the minimum order for swimming equipment? ⌄

Minimum order quantities vary by product. Swimming equipment typically has a MOQ of 20 pieces. Contact us for specific MOQ details per item.

How do I request a quote? ⌄

Click "Get a Free Quote" anywhere on our site, fill out the quote form with your service or product needs, and we'll get back to you. You can also reach us on WhatsApp.

Do you service commercial pools and residential pools? ⌄

Yes. We provide lifeguard services and pool maintenance for both commercial facilities (condominiums, clubs, hotels, schools) and private residential pools.
